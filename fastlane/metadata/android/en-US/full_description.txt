Murmur lets you chat and make voice calls with people nearby over Bluetooth Low Energy, with no
internet, no accounts and no servers. Phones relay messages for each other, so a message can reach
someone out of your own range as long as other Murmur phones sit in between.

The app has no INTERNET permission: it is incapable of sending your data over the network.

Features:
* Radar showing people nearby by signal strength, and people reachable through the mesh.
* #nearby public room, plus named channels with an optional password (encrypted).
* End-to-end encrypted direct messages with delivery and read receipts.
* End-to-end encrypted voice calls over Bluetooth.
* Replies, reactions, @mentions, disappearing messages, waves and an SOS alert.
* Channel invites so friends can join private channels with one tap.
* Optional PIN app lock, favourites, private nicknames, and a full dark theme.
* Demo mode to try the whole app on a single phone.

Murmur stores everything locally and can erase it all with a panic wipe. It is free and
open-source software (Apache-2.0).
